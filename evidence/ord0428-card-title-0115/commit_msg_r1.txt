ORD-0428: the wallet card's headline is a name joined from the plan, not the server's join key

The 预订 tab's `wallet-commitment` card printed `policy.semantic_key` as its `<h2>`, so a confirmed
promise was titled `place-river-cruise` while every other line on that card was already in traveller
words.  `semantic_key` is the key the backend matches on when it binds a commitment to a plan row
(`backend/app/main.py:6128-6144`), not a name.

The name comes from data this screen already holds.  `_commitment_payload`
(`backend/app/main.py:7046`) ships `activity_id`, which is the bound row's id, and the wallet type
declared in `frontend/src/app/V8Experience.tsx` did not carry it -- that omission is why the card had
no way to reach a name and printed the key instead.  A commitment that carries no binding is matched
on the same three fields the backend's own binding used: `place_id`, `city_service_id`, and
`metadata.semantic_key` including its `<place>:<key>` form.  Nothing is invented: a card with nothing
to join to keeps the localised fallback it already had, and the identifier does not reappear on a
`data-*` attribute.

No new mapping table: the badge row keeps using the ORD-0425 shared display layer
(`frontend/src/lib/displayVocabulary.ts`), which ORD-0446 already wired in, and this change adds no
word to it.
