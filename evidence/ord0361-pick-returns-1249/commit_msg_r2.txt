ORD-0361 -r2: the residual half, delivered on the line's own selection -- not a rebase of 11eee48d

`review/S4-ord0361-map-tap-selection` (11eee48da2b117a2c0aafb2d71c50b0104b7d1fb) carried four things:
markers that answer tap/Enter/Space, an unpickable marker that says why, a pick kept per journey, and a
rail whose rows scroll to the picked stop.  Two of those landed underneath it in other orders --
ORD-0390 made the map and the card share one selection and made the markers controls
(`frontend/src/components/v8/MapPanel.tsx:421-434`, `role=button`, `tabIndex`, `click`, Enter and Space,
`aria-pressed`), and ORD-0417 put the picked stop's name on the glass.  Measured on this tip before
touching anything: 6 of 6 markers are controls, tapping marker 3 paints
`图上选中的这一站 · Central Food Hall` and moves the heading to `已选中 · Central Food Hall`, Enter on
marker 1 does the same, 0 console errors.  So the order's sentence -- 地图点选要有可观察后果 -- is met,
and rebasing the old commit would have reintroduced a second selection: it keyed its own
`focusedActivityId` while the line keys on `selectedActivityId`/`detailActivityId`, and the two names
mean different things on the same prop.

What is delivered here is the one half the tip does not do, measured as absent
(`selected_stop_after_reload: null`, storage holding only `citynexus.v8.journey_id`,
`citynexus.v8.trial_draft`, `citynexus.locale` and `citynexus.copilot.history.<journey>`):

  frontend/src/app/V8Experience.tsx -- the picked stop is kept under `citynexus.v8.map_pick.<journeyId>`,
    beside the shell's other journey-scoped references, and restored when that journey's plan is read.
    Keyed by the journey the data belongs to, so a pick cannot cross journeys.  An id that is not a stop
    on this plan is dropped rather than shown: printing a name the plan does not carry is worse than the
    blank it would replace.

  frontend/src/components/v8/MapPanel.tsx -- one real defect found while measuring that restore.  The
    markers are created after the map reports itself ready, from the closure of the render that
    scheduled the build, and that closure still holds the pre-restore selection.  So a selection that
    already existed when the page came up painted every pin `aria-pressed=false` and no `selected`
    class -- the glass named the stop while the pins contradicted it, and nothing repainted them until
    the traveller clicked.  Traced at 0 / 1.5 / 3 / 6 s: all six false, permanently; one click and the
    same pin flips to `true`.  Fixed the way the callback one line above is already fixed, with a ref,
    and the `selected` class is applied on the same path because `map-selection.css:22` makes it the
    visible half of the state.  First attempt at this was a wrong cause -- moving the paint above
    `if (!map) return;` changed nothing -- and that change was reverted rather than kept as decoration.

Measured after, same instrument, same private stack (own backend, own build byte-compared to what the
port serves, own read-only copy of the preview database): three arms x two generations.  Before: pick
restores nothing on any arm.  After: `normal` names `图上选中的这一站 · Old Town Gate` on arrival and
again after reload with the pin painted `true` + `selected`; `foreign` (key written under a different
journey id) restores nothing and both keys coexist; `stale` (an id not on this plan) names nothing and
the entry is gone.  0 console errors on every arm.  Both `tsc` configs report 0 error lines; `vite build`
exits 0.

before any assertion of its own, in isolation as well as in sequence, so it is left out rather than
