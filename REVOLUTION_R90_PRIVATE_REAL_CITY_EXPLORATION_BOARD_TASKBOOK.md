## Revolution product promise
A traveler in a new, zero-catalog city can explore actual sourced POIs, **privately save up to three interesting places**, compare them with factual uncertainty labels, navigate to a public destination on demand with consent, and return later to the draft. This is a usable city exploration board even when admission/opening is not verified and no authoritative PlanVersion exists. It must not masquerade as a confirmed Journey itinerary, route ETA or arrival.
