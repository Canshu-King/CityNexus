[an instrument finding worth keeping]
  fetching a tip by its sha brings the commit without its ancestry.  That made the tip look
  parentless (rev-list 1, no parents) and `merge-tree` answered "refusing to merge unrelated
  histories" -- a refusal that is false here: the histories are shared and merge-base is real.
  Two traps sat on top of it: a shell printing `rc=$?` after a pipe reports the pipe's last
  command rather than git's, and a `head` reading shows only the first lines of a failure.  So
  the tip is always fetched by refspec from here on, and a conflict count is only quoted when
  git itself exits 0.
