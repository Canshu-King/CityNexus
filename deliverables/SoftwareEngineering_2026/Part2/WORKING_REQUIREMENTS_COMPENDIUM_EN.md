## Business goals (8)

### BG-001 Maintain an Executable Journey [M]

**Baseline statement.** For a valid Journey affected by a disruptive event, CityNexus shall produce either an executable revised plan or an explicit no-feasible-plan result.

**Given / when.** Given a valid active Journey and a disruption fixture that makes its current plan infeasible, when recovery is requested

**Expected.** The system returns at least one plan that passes applicable hard-constraint validation or records a no-feasible-plan result with a reason.

**Verification:** demonstration

### BG-002 Reduce Journey Tool Fragmentation [M]

**Baseline statement.** The Golden Journey scenario shall allow a traveler to plan, commit, execute and recover a city journey without manually switching among multiple independent tools to recompute the whole journey.

**Given / when.** Given the defined Golden Journey scenario, when a traveler proceeds from journey creation through execution and disruption recovery

**Expected.** The traveler completes the scenario in CityNexus without manually rebuilding the whole journey in an external planning tool.

**Verification:** demonstration

### BG-003 Recover from Real-World Disruption [M]

**Given / when.** Given separate valid fixtures for place closure, transit delay and weather change, when each event is processed

**Expected.** The system updates journey context and produces an impact decision stating whether replanning is required.

**Verification:** system_test

### BG-004 Protect High-Strength Commitments [M]

**Given / when.** Given an active Journey containing C3 and C4 commitments, when normal replanning is executed

**Expected.** No C4 commitment is moved or removed, and any material C3 impact is disclosed for explicit traveler confirmation.
