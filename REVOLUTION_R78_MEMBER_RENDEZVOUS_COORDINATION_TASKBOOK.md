## Revolution product problem
A real signed newcomer C can now join a living Journey (R76) and privately ask how to reach the *planned* shared stop (R77). But A/B still don't know whether C is choosing to come, needs help, or has changed their mind, unless they use some unrelated channel. A request to calculate a route is private, and must NOT silently become a social status. R62 recovery and R69 meetup handshake mean something different; neither is an "on my way" signal.

**R78 makes the Journey a living group, not a static itinerary:**
> C sees the exact shared stop and (if grounded) personal routing. C taps "Tell my companions I'm on my way" or "I need help coordinating." A/B see a timely, coarse, voluntary, member-attributed status for *that one shared meeting stop*. C can withdraw it; another member joining later does not retroactively inherit the old disclosure. No one sees C's raw GPS coordinates or private route. The shared itinerary does not move, and R69 arrival is not marked merely because C said they were coming.
