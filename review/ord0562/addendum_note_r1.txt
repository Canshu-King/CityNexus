  an anchor's own moment outrank the journey window (backend/app/main.py:3110), so patching the journey
  window left the count at three. The door that moves the anchors is the inbox: a confirmed reservation
  writes its own moments onto the trip's anchors. So this walk uses the product's own two doors --
  POST /api/v1/journeys/{id}/inbox/import (http_status=201) and
  POST /api/v1/journeys/{id}/inbox/{draft_id}/confirm (http_status=200) -- with a same-day rail booking
  whose moments sit inside the stored window (departure 2026-10-08T09:00+08:00, arrival 09:40).
