phone building, cell 4: the AI-planning surface, and a false green of mine retracted

The building's hardest class is 不知道成没成 -- can a person tell whether an action worked?  The honest
case for that is a deployment where AI is off, so this cell presses 「AI 帮我规划」 and pushes all the way
to the point where the user would start a plan.

The finding is that the product is CORRECT here, and that my first two walks were wrong:

Retraction.  My first walk (walk_aiplan) printed VERDICT=TELLABLE -- but its press never landed (the
selector missed the control, so the "screen changed" it measured was just the trip view settling after
the demo tap).  A verdict about a leg that never ran is the exact shape of a fake green, so I replaced it
with an arm that proves each step: press landed -> sheet opens -> the sheet names the cause -> the start
CTA is disabled -> no POST fires -> no spinner.

The arm (evidence/aiplan_tellability_arm.json), all measured on the 390px screen:
- the primary control is enabled and its press is proven to land (elementFromPoint on its centre);
- the sheet opens and says 「这台服务的 AI 开关处于关闭状态，这是运营侧的设置，不是你的操作结果。开关本身
  不代表某次失败的原因。」 -- it separates an operational setting from a user error, which is the honest
  sentence;
- the 「开始规划」 CTA is disabled, so a user cannot start a plan that cannot run; pressing it fires no
  request and leaves no spinner;
- the backend agrees: runtime_status reports NOT_CONFIGURED.

So a person CAN tell where they stand before pressing.  This surface needs no fix.  Two earlier walks of
mine on it are retracted rather than shipped as evidence.
