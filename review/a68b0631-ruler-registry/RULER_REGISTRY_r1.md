## what is NOT here, and why
  The one-off measurement probes in the lane's scratch directory are deliberately not listed: they
  produced a single reading that is quoted in a delivered readings file, and that readings file
  names its own base commit, which is the coordinate for that reading.  A probe that never
  recurs does not need a red-once case to keep a ruler honest.
