`aggregate_provenance` reads null. On this copy the plan-level object has no such key, and the field
that does exist -- inside `active_plan.mobility_evidence` -- reads 'RUNTIME_CAPTURE', not null. So the replacement
sentence was deliberately NOT built on provenance; it is built on the two fields that agree in both

counting them would have kept the informative sentence permanently unreachable.
