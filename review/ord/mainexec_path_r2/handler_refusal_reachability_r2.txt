Read: a handler is the fake-gate shape only if its refusal is an unreachable policy DENY. Handlers
whose action resolves ALLOW_AUTO in every mode (REQUEST_REPLAN, REPORT_DISRUPTION) cannot be refused
by policy; if such a handler also has no other refusal, its only 'gate' is the allowlist. The typed
mutation actions (ACCEPT_REPLAN onward) resolve REQUIRE_* / DENY under the tight envelope, so their
refusals are reachable.
