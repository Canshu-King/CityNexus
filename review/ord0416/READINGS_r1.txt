NAMED MISS -- 320x653 does not reach the 10-row bar and this lane did not tune the rule for it
  before 2, after 8. It is an improvement of 6 rows, but the acceptance said not below 10, so the
  cell is reported red by name. The reason is geometric, not the icon: the viewport is 653 px tall
  while the hit floor is 768 px, so from row 9 down the centre is below the screen and
  elementFromPoint returns null. Read row by row at that shape:
    row 1..8 centres 137,198,259,346,407,468,555,616 -> hittable and within floor
    row 9..14 centres 703,764,825,886,947,1008 -> top element is null (outside the viewport)
  Fitting 10 rows there needs 115 px of head/heading + 10*61 px of row advance = 725 px of visible
  stack against a 653 px viewport. The bar and the shape are incompatible; the choice is the
  commander's: relax the bar for viewports shorter than the floor, or measure that shape against
  min(viewport_height, floor) instead of the frozen 768. This lane changed neither.

THE OTHER THREE SHAPES clear the bar: 390x844 2->10, 360x800 2->10, 414x896 2->10, and zh/en read
identically in every cell, so the two arms are the same 档 as required.

A SIBLING DEFECT THE SCREENSHOTS EXPOSED, NOT FIXED HERE
  Looking at the after arm's own screenshot, the drawer's rows render as filled indigo buttons with
  white text, and the before arm's screenshot shows the same -- so it is pre-existing, not caused by
  this change. Cause is the same family: styles.css:331 `#v8-nav-drawer > button { ... background:
  transparent; color: #758196 ... }` never matches either, nor do :337 (`> button:hover,
  > button.active`) and :338 (`> button.active`). Three dead rules, one wrapper <div>.
  Not proposed silently as part of ORD-0416: turning 14 filled buttons into 14 transparent ones is a
  visible design change beyond the order's scope, and the exposure gate is the commander's call.
  Ask: rule a companion order (same descendant fix for the three button rules), or say the filled
  look is intended and this lane leaves it.
