   no sentence appears. To measure the positive arm a plan was written into THIS LANE'S database
   (`_tip_render.db`, ports 4891/4892) through the model: three stops, two pointing at place rows that
   exist, one with `place_id` NULL, plus one catalog-hydration job whose stored attempts say photon
   answered-nothing and nominatim never answered. The row is a fixture for a render test, not a supply
   claim: the product has no door that creates `place_id` NULL-with-a-dangling-reference, because
   `activities.place_id` is ON DELETE SET NULL, which is also why the projection's other reason
   (COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE) cannot be reached on this screen and is left unmeasured rather than forced.
   Two repairs were needed to my own staging before any of this was readable, and both are named here
   because they looked like product failures until they were not:

     - a plan id that was not a UUID -> the response model refused `active_plan_version_id`, and
       `GET /journeys/<id>/plans` and `/twin` answered 500. With legal ids all four routes answer 200:

3. THE DEFECT THIS READING FOUND, AND THE ONE LINE THAT FIXES IT
   On the pre-fix bundle the dark tier read: fg=245,245,247 on bg=255,255,255 -> 对比 **1.09**, 需 4.5 -> 不过档：白底白字。
   Cause: the card that holds the sentence carried a hardcoded surface, unlike every other card --
     旧: --v8-line); border-radius: var(--cn-r-12); background: #fff;
     新: order-radius: var(--cn-r-12); background: var(--cn-surface);
   After the change, same cell, same four arms: light 16.83 / dark 15.63 (需 4.5), both pass.
   The before number is recomputable rather than remembered: the removed declaration is re-imposed as
   an author style on the current bundle and measured again -- labelled as an EMULATION of the old rule,
   not a reading of an old artifact. It yields 1.09 again, and light stays at 16.83.
   No second colour scale was introduced: `--cn-surface` is the token the other cards already take
   (light #ffffff, dark #1c1c1e), and the change is one insertion and one deletion in one selector.
