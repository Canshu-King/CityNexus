3. THE DEFECT THE READING FOUND, AND THE ONE DECLARATION THAT FIXES IT
   Before the change the dark tier put前景 245,245,247 on 背景 255,255,255 -> 对比 1.09 against a required 4.5 -- the
   sentence was there in the DOM and unreadable on screen. Light was fine at 16.83.
   Cause: the card holding it hardcoded its surface where every comparable card takes the token --
     old: .v8-map-facts background #fff   ->   new: .v8-map-facts background var(--cn-surface)
   `--cn-surface` is the token already declared once per scheme (design-tokens.css:11 light #ffffff,
   design-tokens.css:168 dark #1c1c1e): no new scale, no new variable, one insertion and one deletion
   in frontend/src/styles.css.
   After it, the same four arms read 16.83 (light) and 15.63 (dark) at both shapes -- both over the
   required 4.5 tier, box [310, 42] px at the phone shape with the centre hit-testing to itself.
   The before figure is recomputable rather than remembered: the removed declaration is re-imposed as
   an author style on the delivered bundle and measured again. The artifact labels that arm EMULATION
   of the old rule -- it is the old rule applied to today's element, not a reading of an old bundle --
   and it yields 1.09 again, light still 16.83.
