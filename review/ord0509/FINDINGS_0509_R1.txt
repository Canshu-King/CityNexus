4. THE MUTANT
   e2e/qoder-ord0509-contrast-screens.spec.ts ends with a role-stripping arm on the poster screen:
   it reads the roles the DOM declares, scans, removes every data-contrast-role, scans again, and
   requires the row count to go UP with at least one unclassified-control row. It is one of the two
   arms that pass in the run; the receipts are in review/ord0509/run/mutant.role-hidden.json.
   Its first version failed for a reason worth keeping: it clicked at zero milliseconds after
   navigation, found no roles because nothing had mounted, and would have asserted against an
   empty list. A 900 ms wait is what makes it measure the screen instead of the race.
