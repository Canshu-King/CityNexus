phone building, cell 2: the product fix and its before/after arm

The walk (cell 1) found the plan-preview day-list printing server role words in English inside a Chinese
row.  This cell ships the fix and the arm that shows it biting in both directions.

Fix: `refs/heads/review/0afdb49b-ord0500-preview-language-r1` head `b5a86184` (parent = the live tip it was
cut on), two product files: `frontend/src/lib/displayVocabulary.ts` (the new `activityWordLabel`) and
`frontend/src/app/V8Experience.tsx` (the two render sites that bypassed the role table).

The measured detail that made the first attempt fail: the strings are COMPOUND -- the ground truth read
off the live `/experience` payload is `Check in · Beijing South Courtyard`, not a bare role word, so
feeding the whole token to `roleLabel` matches nothing and the English survives.  `activityWordLabel`
therefore splits on `·` and maps each segment, and only where the role table actually names it
(`roleLabel(v) !== humanizeToken(v)`), so a place title is returned verbatim.

The arm (evidence/walk_preview_language_arm.json): two renders of the same manual booking on the same
tip, only the served bundle differs.  Before: the three English words present, the Chinese absent.
After: the English gone, 入住 / 退房 / 过夜 present, and every place name unchanged in both.  Both booking
paths (demo and manual) still reach BOOKED with 0 page errors and 0 console errors.
