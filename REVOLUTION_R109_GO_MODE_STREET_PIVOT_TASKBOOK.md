## Product revolution: when life changes, the travel companion adapts
The rigid two-stop GO plan is useful until the second place is closed, too far, or the traveler changes their mind. CityNexus must offer a natural, **human-controlled mid-walk pivot** rather than wiping the first-stop self-report and making them start over.

**User story:** I told CityNexus that I finished exploring Stop 1. My planned Stop 2 does not work. I tap **Change my second stop**, see a third *saved* public-source place and what is still unknown, choose it, explicitly confirm, and now GO MODE says **Stop 1 self-reported done; next action: head to NEW Stop 2**. No GPS or external calls are implied. I can reload and keep the updated two-stop identity, with both the preserved self-report and the changed destination clearly marked. The alternative's walkability/ETA remains UNKNOWN unless an existing valid route fact or separately consented route request establishes it.
