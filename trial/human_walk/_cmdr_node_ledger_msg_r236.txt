Roll the whole red set into one ledger keyed by node id, split by which side a fix belongs to.

Rulings were being asked for bucket by bucket while the buckets were only counts.  This joins
pytest's crash lines to its FAILED summary -- both lists enforced against the run's own failed
number, so the pairing cannot slip -- applies today's three measured refinements (the KeyError rows
that are really the bearer-less family, and the classroom/demo refusals measured under the
environment arms), and writes every node id under its cause with a fixture-side versus
product/seed-side split: 130 against 101, summing to the run's own 231.

The ruler also caught a defect in itself: the first node-id pattern dropped parametrised case
suffixes, collapsing 231 failures to 218 distinct ids, and it aborted rather than publishing a
silently deduplicated ledger.  That is written into the report so the next instrument does not
repeat it.  Nothing here was repaired -- the point is to let one ruling cover the fixture-side mass
instead of fifteen separate ones.
