Sixteen reds in four files are one fixture family: private reads issued without a bearer.

An observation-only probe wrapped TestClient.get and counted every /experience and /events read in
tests/test_r64_member_side_quest.py, tests/stateful/test_journey_truth_state_machine.py,
tests/test_provider_runtime_gate.py and tests/test_qoder_activity_identity_contract.py: 44 reads,
none carrying a credential, every one answered 401 with a body whose only top-level key is error.
A control in the same arm reads the same journey twice, and the two shapes differ: unsigned gives
401 {error}, signed gives 200 with active_plan present, and for /events unsigned hands back a dict
whose iterated members are strings, which is exactly the AttributeError those ten reds carry.
So the earlier "2 KeyError, another class, unexplored" line was an undercount of the same family;
the contract never renamed the key and the product still emits it unconditionally.
No product byte moved, and the fixture repair itself is not taken: it exceeds knife three's scope.
