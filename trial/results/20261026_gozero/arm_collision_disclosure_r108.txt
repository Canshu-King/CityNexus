WHY THIS EXISTS
Three payments in one session, all from deriving a new walk arm by copying an old one and renaming only
part of it:
  1. _ba_arm_switch_on_draft_430_r103.mjs and the first _bb_arm_three_labels_430_r104.mjs were cp-derived
     from _az_arm_layer_switch_430_r102.mjs, which left them opening the profile _chrome_gozero_az102.
     bb104 died on the profile lock; ba103 produced readings from a second page inside a browser az102 was
     still driving.  Those readings looked like an independent walk and were not.
  2. _aq_arm_clean_430_r92.mjs's own profile was renamed to _chrome_gozero_aq99, but five later arms in the
     same chain still declared _chrome_gozero_aq92.
  3. Three receipt names were written by two arms each, so whichever ran second overwrote the first.
